OUR STORY

WHY BY ELEVEN:A HOUSE OFELEVEN CRAFTS

THE ART of THE IDEA, THE CRAFT of THE JOURNEY

Our name is our story. BY is a signature: every brand that leaves our house carries the care of the people who shaped it. ELEVEN is our pillar. Written as a number, it is two pillars standing side by side: a gate between vision and reality, and the strength everything we build rests on.

PEOPLE WORKING with PEOPLE

Behind every brand there are people, and we believe the best work happens when people work with people. Like horse and rider, we move as one with the brands we carry, your team and ours, side by side. Eleven crafts under one roof, one direction, and a promise to take you further than you could go alone.
