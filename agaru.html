AGARU SPORTS · SHORT-FORM REEL CAMPAIGN

Lost in thelandscape.

A colourway born from the heat. For Agaru's gradient drop we took the collection out of the studio and into the mountains, where sun, stone and silence could tell the story better than any backdrop ever could. Told as a sequence of reels, each one a chapter of the same journey, released frame by frame across the drop.

BURGUNDY

THE STORY,in three acts

The fade of the garment became the script for the whole campaign. One athlete, one colour, and a landscape vast enough to make both unforgettable.

01
THE PALETTE Red gradient, warm stone, open sky.

The garment fades from deep burgundy to dusty rose, the way late light falls across the rock. We built every frame, every tone and every grade around that single fade.

02
THE SETTING Rugged mountains. A sense of exploration.

An athlete navigating unfamiliar terrain, almost lost in the scale of it. Heat, harsh sun and raw stone shape the story, so the colour becomes the one thing you cannot look away from.

03
THE DROP A sequence of reels.

A short-form campaign told in chapters. Each reel revealed a little more of the journey, building anticipation across the feed until the full colourway landed on launch day.

The colour was the hero. The landscape simply made room for it.
