THE BRIEF

Private aviation has always spoken in whispers. Altara Privé wanted to be heard.

For three months, at the very beginning of the brand, we explored how a private aviation house could feel closer without lowering its standard. We started with the concepts: what flying private means to someone who has never done it, and what they would need to know to imagine it for themselves.

Then we brought the mood and the language down to earth. Softer images, warmer words, clearer answers. Content that informs before it impresses, written to reach a far wider audience, in a voice that sounds like a conversation rather than a closed door.

Less velvet rope.More open door.

The standard stays exactly where it was. The door is simply open now, with someone there to explain what waits inside.

I.

THE TAKE-OFF

Every flight begins on the ground. Before a single post, we thought through the concepts the brand would stand on: access, honesty and ease, each one a way of turning a closed world into a story people could step into.

II.

THE LANDING

We translated mood and language for a broader public. Less distance, more information, a friendlier voice. Premium in every detail, yet finally speaking to the people it had never addressed.

A NOTE ON THE PROJECT

This was a three-month engagement at the very start of the brand's journey. The project stopped after this first chapter, but the thinking behind it, the concepts, the mood and the voice, remains work we are proud to show.
